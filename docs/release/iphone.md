# iPhone interface

On iPhone the touch interface adapts to a compact layout in portrait and
landscape. It has been tested on the desktop with simulated phone screen sizes
and scripted input, but not yet as a signed build on a physical iPhone.

| Control | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Tools | Open the scrolling tool drawer. Choosing a tool closes it so you can touch the city. Tap Tools again (or Close tools) to dismiss it. Terrain tools remain available while shaping a new city. |
| Inspect | Select tile inspection in a founded or loaded city, then touch and release a tile. In portrait camera layouts, Inspect is inside Menu. |
| Menu | Open the City, View, Speed, Disasters, Reports and Help commands. |
| Details / Alerts | Expand the footer's full metrics and warnings. Less collapses it. The compact footer keeps treasury, date and the current tool visible. |
| Explore | Use the left movement pad and drag on the right to look. The right-side actions adapt to walking, driving or flying. Menu pauses movement; Resume continues. |

On notched iPhones in portrait, Tools and Menu sit beside the camera and the
menu background reaches the top edge. The camera's center stays clear, without
an extra menu row underneath it. The portrait camera band is 60 points high,
with equal 8-point spacing above and below its 44-point buttons; a larger OS
inset can expand the band while keeping the buttons centered.
Landscape keeps actions clear of the side
camera while filling the bar to the display edges. Narrow phones retain a
single safe-area action row. iPad keeps its existing menu layout.

Reports stack columns and turn financial and industry tables into labeled rows.
All their numeric fields and actions still work. Scroll vertically to read
long reports; dialog titles and actions stay outside the body scroll. Expanded
status details also scroll on short screens. The docked
keyboard reduces the available height. Rotation closes the tool drawer and
cancels held gestures; Explore requires Resume after a display change.

Active Explore uses the phone's safe area: city menus and the footer return
when you tap Menu or return to Build. Map is a compact button beside Menu;
it opens the paused browsing map. The movement pad and context actions stay
at the lower edges, leaving the world clear. Menu also shows full status,
objectives and touch instructions. Train doors and departure guidance take
priority over long station names in the compact status.

Casino tables use a small header, concise treasury and bet readout, and a chip
denomination chooser. Choosing a denomination does not bet: + places that chip
and − removes it. The betting selector provides precise selection of small
roulette, faro, wheel and dice spots, with the selected stake and payout odds.
Results remain on the game surface. Rules retains full game terms, commentary
and the sitting's result. Large-stake confirmations and errors stay in a bounded
scrolling feedback area. iPad keeps the larger resort header and
individual chip buttons.

The Build minimap is hidden on phone layouts. Returning to an iPad-sized
layout restores its saved preference.
Files, suspension recovery, construction gestures and hardware controls work as described in
the [iOS touch and Files guide](ipad.md). In Files, use **On My iPhone →
SC2D** to copy a city into its Documents folder before browsing.

The iOS preset includes both the iPhone and iPad device families. Testing on a
physical iPhone should cover real safe areas and keyboards, how the touch
controls feel, rotation, Files, background recovery and performance in
representative cities.
