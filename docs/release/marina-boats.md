# Marina boats

In Explore City, walk up to a marina from its shore access. The boarding prompt
appears nearby. Use **Interact** on touch screens, or the configured interaction
key (**F** by default), to board a sailboat in the marina's open-water berth.

The movement pad controls throttle and steering; **Brake** stops the boat.
Keyboard movement and braking use the normal Explore bindings. To choose
**Harbor Ship** or **Sailboat**, open the Explore menu while walking near the
marina and select **Drive selected vehicle**.

Stop beside a marina berth and use Interact to return to its clear, supported
shore access. A stopped boat can also exit beside a safe shoreline. Open water
has no walking exit. A marina needs connected navigable water, a clear berth,
and a usable bank; blocked access keeps the player in their current mode.

Boats and boarding belong to the Explore session. Sailing never changes the
city, its funds, the simulation or the saved city file.
