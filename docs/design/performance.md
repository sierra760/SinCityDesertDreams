# Rendering and simulation performance

The project uses Godot’s **Mobile** rendering backend (native Metal on supported Macs). Procedural palette colors are converted from sRGB to linear for lighting; the Compatibility fallback shades in sRGB and skips that conversion.

The **High** 3D profile preserves full shadows, four-sample antialiasing and close-up model detail. **City → Settings…** also offers **Balanced** (two-sample antialiasing, a shorter shadow range and earlier mesh detail reduction) and **Performance** (shadows off and earlier detail reduction). The separate **Resolution** control offers 100%, 75% and 50% 3D rendering; interface text keeps its native resolution. First-run defaults depend on the device (`platform/mobile_platform.gd`): desktops start on High at 100%, or at 75% when the display's backing scale is 2 or more; Windows, Linux and BSD machines with an integrated GPU start on Balanced; iOS and Android start on Balanced at 75%. A choice the player has made is never replaced by these defaults. These preferences persist across launches and do not modify city data. Camera projection and mouse picking account for the selected resolution.

## Runtime strategy

- City geometry is kept in spatial regions. Raw city layer writes are detected, while utility-only flags do not rebuild static meshes. Lot footprints, terrain neighbors and whole bridge spans participate in invalidation.
- Network regions compare their actual projection inputs. A building change that leaves the network untouched preserves its geometry. Walking surfaces are built only when exploration requests a traversal snapshot; each returned snapshot describes one completed geometry revision.
- Dense curved-road collision extraction indexes bounding boxes while keeping the same clipping, wall tests and output order. Curved drawing reuses local clipping templates and samples repeated points once per invocation. Hidden Underground projection is deferred until requested.
- Binding another city with exactly matching detached geometry inputs reuses the completed meshes and traversal snapshot. Live presentation layers still bind the new City; different geometric inputs use a fresh projection.
- Compatible static model leaves are compiled once into shared runtime meshes. Opaque geometry gets a shared shadow-only proxy. Materials, physical structures, editable assets and querying are unaffected. Content with custom shaders, animation or transparency is rendered normally instead.
- Distant procedural palm crowns use lower-detail mesh indices. Near geometry keeps full detail. Spatial mesh batches preserve useful culling and can update affected regions.
- Traffic meshes and upload buffers are reused. Power-warning updates skip work when their input bytes have not changed.
- Explore keeps retained detailed actors visible while sampling their current
  poses, avoiding recursive hide/show work on every frame. Owner animation ticks
  check selected IDs directly without building debug snapshots or searching a
  selection array; detail distances, models and motion clocks stay the same.
- The minimap retains its overlay drawing commands while camera projection,
  map layout and Explore marker are unchanged, and skips hidden overlay work.
  Port updates reuse completed zone membership for the same city and detached
  zone bytes while recounting current buildings and services; crane growth
  forces canonical regrouping even when the water was already zoned.
- iCloud inventory compares each remote record against one local publication
  head instead of copying the entire save library for every record. Payloads,
  history and upload pins stay outside this unchanged-record comparison.
- Tourism district discovery keeps complete sites for detached building/zone inputs. Native value searches collect relevant buildings and all commercial corner-byte variants, then restore row-major order. Service, roads, fire, terrain and flood eligibility still update during simulation hooks. Service-only transport refreshes reuse the detached building classification while retaining gateway and route-version updates.
- Commuters share ordered transition arrays within one bound transport pass. Both binding boundaries discard them; destination checks, route costs, traffic and search-work limits keep their original order. Agricultural growth similarly searches zone bytes natively and sorts candidates instead of walking unrelated city tiles in script.
- Explore rain and dust keep their particle resources through daylight/fog blends and owner changes with identical presentation. Wind changes refresh existing poses; precipitation and quality changes rebuild the particle set. Frozen active time reuses particle transforms, while pre-draw still updates indoor visibility. A newly created or resized buffer receives its first pose even while paused.
- Interactive simulation checks a four-millisecond work budget between complete days, admitting at most eight days per frame. A single day can exceed that budget. Pending elapsed time is saved, and resuming preserves unfinished simulation work. Pauses and modal holds are checked between days. Slow, Medium and Fast schedule one day every 0.8, 0.32 and 0.08 seconds. Fastest admits complete days immediately within the frame limits, without a timer delay; it neither adds nor spends elapsed-time backlog. Earlier backlog survives saves and resumes when a paced speed is selected. Its nominal 0.08-second duration remains available to vehicle/disaster interpolation. Direct headless day advancement measures CPU throughput independently of interactive pacing.
- Loading a city builds each network, terrain and lot owner once from shared inputs: one read-only ground sampling scope spans change detection, water styling and all geometry owners; deck/approach/grade profiles are computed once per exact input set and shared by the renderer, traffic graphs and street topologies; the save decoder's validated road topology is adopted by the host instead of being projected again. Onramp surfaces evaluate each lateral height profile and each step direction once and combine them per corner; physical patches are packed arrays rather than one record per triangle. The output is identical to computing each corner and record separately (`game/tests/test_network_builder_exactness.gd`, `game/tests/test_network_layer_golden.gd`).
- Compiled model scenes are cached under `user://compiled-models/` on desktop and under the operating system's cache directory on iOS and Android, so the regenerable cache stays out of the player's Files-visible, backed-up documents. Entries are keyed by model code, shadow mode, the catalog's GLB hash, the compiler version and the engine version. A warm cache instantiates models without loading or compiling the source GLB; damaged or stale entries recompile and are rewritten. The cache embeds equal copies of the import's materials and shapes; `game/tests/test_compiled_model_cache.gd` verifies geometry, LODs, shadows, materials and collision.
- During play, growth and construction refresh only what changed: building/zone edits recolor the existing terrain chunk's sand and skirt triangles instead of rebuilding it (tunnel mouths and portals still rebuild; flags never touch terrain unless a rail bridge orients a portal); touched lots whose complete inputs are unchanged keep their nodes; the static mesh batcher keeps per-branch inventories and remakes only the groups whose membership changed, reusing batch nodes; the minimap repaints changed tiles; street-name reconciliation and the street topology project only network codes and skip when their inputs are unchanged; a network region rebuilt for palms or colors keeps the resolved physical surfaces; the traffic graph treats ground cover as land use. Each shortcut produces the same result as a full rebuild (`game/tests/test_terrain_recolor_exactness.gd`, `game/tests/test_city_mesh_batcher_city_equivalence.gd`, `game/tests/test_city_lot_retention.gd`, `game/tests/test_minimap_incremental.gd`, `game/tests/test_street_naming_reconcile_skip.gd`, `game/tests/test_street_topology_structural_inputs.gd`, `game/tests/test_network_physics_cache_retention.gd`, `game/tests/test_traffic_graph_land_use_kinds.gd`, `game/tests/test_traversal_ceiling_cache.gd`).
- GDScript threads do not parallelize this work in the current engine build: twelve pure-compute WorkerThreadPool tasks took 7.5× one task's wall time. Heavy kernels are reduced algorithmically or moved to native code instead.

## Repeatable checks

`game/tools/golden_networks.gd` prints the complete chunked network-layer output hashes of each city path argument; refresh `game/tests/test_network_layer_golden.gd` from it only after a deliberate geometry change.

Focused regression coverage includes incremental/full rebuild equivalence, immutable traversal snapshots, compiled mesh/shadow equivalence, graphics preference roundtrips and reduced-resolution picking, resource reuse, exact scheduled simulation results, and backlog save/reload.

Simulation cache regressions compare full-scan district discovery, cold transport graphs, legacy commuter paths/work counts and full-scan farm eligibility, including raw layer edits and city rebinds (`test_tourism_site_reuse.gd`, `test_tourism_structure_reuse.gd`, `test_transport_transition_reuse.gd`, `test_agricultural_growth_sparse_work.gd`).
