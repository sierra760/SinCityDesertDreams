Real-world terrain data sources

The game resamples elevation and water, rotates the selected area, adjusts relief, smooths when requested and repairs heights for playable city terrain. These modifications are by Bristlecone Artists LLC, not endorsed by the data providers. The square uses a spherical approximation: distances and shapes are approximate. Water reflects 2021 land cover and can miss narrow channels or differ from today's shoreline. This is a game landscape, not a navigation or survey map.
